Book a cleaning

Answer a few questions about your home, see your price straight away, then choose a date and a one hour arrival window. It takes about a minute.

What you will be asked

The type of cleaning you need, the size of your home, how many bathrooms it has, and how often you would like us. Your price appears before you give us any personal details. After that we ask for your name, phone number, service address, and your preferred date and time.

About text messages

On the step where you enter your phone number you will see a consent checkbox. It is not ticked by default and opting in is completely optional. You can finish your booking either way.

  • We send booking confirmations and appointment details only
  • We never send marketing or promotional messages
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help

No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions and SMS opt-in details.
