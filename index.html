Text messages

How we use text messaging

When you book a cleaning you can choose to receive text message updates about that booking. On the booking form you enter your phone number and are shown a consent checkbox that is not ticked by default.

Opting in is completely optional. You can complete your booking without it, and you will not receive any text messages if you do not opt in.

  • We send booking confirmations and appointment details only
  • We never send marketing or promotional text messages
  • Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help

No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Full details are on our SMS opt-in page and in our Privacy Policy.

Questions

Good to know

How is my price worked out?

By the size of your home, how many bathrooms it has, the type of clean you pick and how often you want us. You see the full price on screen before you give us any details.

Do I have to be home?

No. Plenty of customers leave a key or a door code. Whatever suits you.

What if my home is bigger than the sizes listed?

Anything over 6,000 sq ft gets a custom quote. Start the questionnaire and we will come back to you with a price.

How do I pay?

Payment is due after the cleaning has started. We accept Venmo, Cash App and Zelle. We do not accept cash, checks, or credit/debit cards.

Can I change or cancel a booking?

Yes. Give us a call on the number at the top of the page and we will move it for you.
